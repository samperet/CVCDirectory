# Proposal-Forming

The sociocratic proposal-forming steps our community follows. In the app, a
proposal is put to a circle (Documents → **New** → **A proposal**, a page's
**Propose for consent**, or **Add a proposal** in a page's editor), and the
circle consents to it at one of its meetings — from the meeting's notes, whose
**Consent** button records who was there, the circle, and who recorded it (see
the README, Documents → Proposals). The steps below are the work that happens
before and around that meeting.

**1. Present the issue.** *(A problem or opportunity)*

**2. Identify aspects of the issue.** *(Elements, dimensions, categories)*

- No solutions at this stage.
- This step is only about understanding the problem.

**3. Consent to completeness of the list.**

**4. Proposal ideas.**

- 4a. Generate proposal ideas in rounds, incl. ways to measure & evaluate the implemented proposal.
- 4b. Each aspect of the issue should have one or more proposal ideas (& ways to measure & evaluate).

*Remember:* don't judge or evaluate ideas — this is about generating free-flow ideas.

**5. Organize ideas.**

- 5a. Select "Tuners." (People who organize information without changing its meaning.)
- 5b. Tuners shape ideas into one or more manageable proposal(s).
- 5c. Circulate proposal(s) to circle.

*Remember:* 5b can be done at a break or separate session. It is important that
Tuners stay neutral when organizing collected information.

**6. Consent Round: Does (each) proposal address all identified aspects of the issue?**

If all circle members give consent, the proposal is now complete. (Not *passed*,
but an official proposal.)

If there are one or more objections, ask *which* aspect of the proposal does not
address one of the identified aspects of the issue, and add a piece to the
proposal that addresses it.

Repeat consent rounds until all aspects of the proposal are addressed. When
everyone in the circle gives consent, the proposal is complete.
